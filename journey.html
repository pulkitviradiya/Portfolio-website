The moment

The Delhi store moment

₹2,500 spent in a store I didn’t plan to enter

Walked into a Delhi store after seeing a long queue outside. Walked out spending ₹2,500 on products I didn’t know I needed. That single question, what just happened in there?, sent me deep into consumer behaviour, impulse buying, and how retail experiences are designed.

Surat · The 53rd store

Became an early franchise partner, the 53rd store, while Lenskart was still an early-stage startup. Built and scaled a 290 sq ft flagship, growing monthly revenue from ₹3L to ₹15L+.

Spent 11 years inside a scaling startup, learning how fast-moving consumer brands experiment, optimise, and evolve across pricing, product, and customer experience. Launched a second franchise unit in Gandhidham in 2020.
