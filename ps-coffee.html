The context

Why should a good cup require a detour?

The venture thesis starts with a gap between specialty coffee as a sit-down occasion and coffee as an everyday routine. P.S. Coffee is being built to serve the latter in Ahmedabad.

The full background

Ahmedabad already has a generation of urban coffee drinkers. What it doesn't have is the infrastructure to support a daily habit. The specialty options that exist are sit-down experiences, occasions rather than routines, priced and placed in ways that don't fit into a typical workday.

P.S. Coffee is being built to close that gap. Compact Pods, 50–200 sqft formats, embedded directly where people already spend their time: corporate campuses, co-working spaces, gyms, and high-footfall street corners. No seating, no dwell time, just great coffee, fast.

The product is 100% Arabica specialty coffee alongside ceremonial-grade matcha, served through a tight, repeatable menu designed for speed and consistency at the Pod level. The whole thesis is that quality and convenience aren't a tradeoff. We're pre-launch and currently raising a proof round.

The operating detail

Behind the
headline.

The infrastructure gap

Ahmedabad has the coffee drinker. What it lacks is the daily-habit infrastructure. Specialty options exist, but they're sit-down, occasion-driven, and not built for the person who just wants a great cup on the way to their desk.

The Pod format

50–200 sqft, no seating. Pods are embedded inside environments where the customer already is: corporate campuses, co-working spaces, gyms, busy corners. The location is the distribution strategy.

Specialty product

100% Arabica specialty coffee and ceremonial-grade matcha, served through a tight, repeatable menu. Quality is non-negotiable; the format enforces speed and consistency at every Pod.

Routine, not occasion

Most specialty coffee in India is priced and placed as a treat. P.S. Coffee is designed to fit inside someone's day without interrupting it, a habit-forming price point, a grab-and-go format, and locations that require zero detour.

Launch fleet of 5 Pods

The proof round is designed to validate unit economics across 5 Pods in Ahmedabad, testing location types, throughput, and menu performance before scaling the model.

Pre-launch, raising now

Currently in pre-launch phase, raising a proof round to fund the initial Pod fleet. The build is being documented publicly at pscoffee.in, including the brand, the thesis, and the progress.

What stays with me

Make quality part of the routine.
